# Bruno A. Freitas

**Political economy of public contracts.** I study how political institutions shape the design, competition, and performance of complex public contracts — using public-private partnerships (PPPs) and concessions as my laboratory. Doctoral researcher at **Insper** — research line *Strategy in Public & Third-Sector Organizations* (advisor: Sergio G. Lazzarini) — and **senior consultant in PPPs & concessions** at **Radar PPP**.

### 📌 Selected work

- **Do Parties Matter for Public-Private Partnerships? Evidence from Close Mayoral Elections in Brazil** — SSRN preprint, 2026 · [doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7112699](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7112699) (from the master's dissertation). A sharp RDD on 3,601 close mayoral elections: a precise, equivalence-tested null on partisan effects in PPP adoption.
- **Who Gets to Compete? Institutional Barriers to Entry and Competition in Brazilian PPP Auctions** — SSRN preprint, 2026 · [doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7112898](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7112898). 139 public-lighting PPP contracts (2013–2025): how procurement design relates to the number of bidders (conditional associations).
- **Politics after Contracting: renegotiation and the political economy of long-term public contracts** — in progress, supervised by Sergio G. Lazzarini.
- **Credible Nulls in Close-Election Regression Discontinuity Designs** — under review.
- **A Reforma Gerencial e as Parcerias Público-Privadas no Brasil: a ideologia político-partidária importa?** — — under review.

➡️ **Latest work:** [SSRN author page](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/cf_dev/AbsByAuth.cfm?per_id=12338348) · full list on [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0009-0002-4130-7140) and [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Rp4JDGsAAAAJ&hl=pt-BR).

### 🔬 Current lines of work

- **Political ideology and the adoption of PPPs** in Brazilian municipalities (evidence from close elections).
- **Politics after contracting** — renegotiation and post-contractual dynamics.
- **Institutional barriers to entry** in PPP auctions (who gets to compete?).
- **Credible nulls** in close-election RDD — treating null results as findings, with equivalence tests.
- **Measuring ideology with LLMs** — a methodological note + a public, DOI-minted corpus.

### 🧭 Methods

Causal inference and quasi-experimental design (regression discontinuity, difference-in-differences, IV, synthetic control), applied econometrics, and computational text analysis — in R and Stata.

### 🧠 Research philosophy

I believe empirical research should be transparent, reproducible, and connected to real policy problems. My aim is evidence that improves both academic understanding and public decision-making.
